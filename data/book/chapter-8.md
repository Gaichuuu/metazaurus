## Summary

### Love's Fishing & Sam's Return

Love is fishing, and they encounter a Beastie fish native to the region. Two weeks have gone by since Sam left for solo training, for a total of six weeks. While Love hasn't seen Sam, he's certainly heard and felt him as he developed his training and special moves. It's been long enough, and loud enough, that Love has considered going out to find Sam so they can continue on their way.

In the past two weeks, Love has done some exploring of his own, and come across the symbols and totems of a Pukwudgie tribe—likely an offshoot of the North East Kingdom, maybe outcasts due to some internal conflict. Whatever the case, they would likely tolerate his and Sam's presence for only a little while longer. Not to mention he's found tufts of dog hair on branches, clearly the sign of some sort of Dogman marking its territory.

The Beasties in the region must be on edge given whatever was happening in Cincinnati and the Convoy that has been circling the city for the past six weeks. Love has seen the lights of both the Convoy and the Spirit Storm (that must still be raging) on the horizon almost every night, getting closer and closer—this adds to his sense of urgency.

Just then, a massive Peninsula Python jumps out of the Ohio River—it is twenty feet long, at least, and coils next to Love, ready to strike. Love isn't frightened at all, and right before it strikes, it is hit by a Lightning Bolt from Dingbelle and passes out in the water. Love laughs and thanks Dingbelle, and states that its own training is coming along nicely.

Love says that he best be on his way to retrieve Sam, and right as he gets up and dusts himself off, Sam emerges from the forest, baseball bat in hand. Love notices that the past two weeks have been rough on Sam—his underfed, wiry muscles, and scuff marks, scratches, and bruises all over his body. But Sam is smiling, clearly satisfied with the progress he's made and eager to show Love.

Dingbelle jumps on Sam, there's some laughter. Love says they will have to find him some grub, and then he wants Sam to show him what he's learned. Sam is queasy at the prospect of Love preparing a meal for him.

### The Soldiers

Just as his stomach growls, there is a whistle in the background that sounds like a bird at first, but is then answered by another call. Sam begins to ask what kind of Beastie makes that sound, and Love hushes him before saying, "That's no Beastie." The sound is that of a cardinal.

As he says this, four soldiers emerge from the thicket—two of whom have their own Beasties beside them and Spellbooks at the ready, and a third with an iron rod. The fourth holds back, arms crossed.

Dingbelle essentially growls and Sam puts him down, hushing him, telling him that it's alright. Sam steps up to the plate.

One of the soldiers sizes up Sam, tells him to stop, and announces that they are the forward party of the Cincinnati Convoy. He demands to know their business. Sam tells him that they are simply passing through for some light training.

Be that as it may, the soldier says that all Casters between the age of sixteen and thirty-five have been drafted for the Convoy and the larger war effort, and accuses Sam of draft-dodging and demands that he and his Contracts (which causes Love to snort) come with them immediately.

Sam whispers under his breath, "What's with all you assholes trying to recruit me for your dumb war." Soldier two demands he speaks up, and Sam says out loud, "I said... I'm not joining your dumb army, yours or anyone else's."

### Sam's Demonstration

The soldiers decide to take Sam by force. Sam moves forward and Love tells him that he can demonstrate his techniques on these soldiers, but that he's not to kill them.

Sam says he has a little bit more control than that—he's not going to kill anyone. He just wishes he could eat one of Love's bug burgers before he fought.

As Sam runs off, Love warns him against attacking the soldier in the back, as Love is getting a funny feeling from him.

One of the soldiers has a Giant Salamander (Flame) as his Contract, the other has a Billdad (Forest), and the third has a rod which he electrifies.

Sam runs at them and jumps clear over their heads, to the surprise of the soldiers (physical strength). While he's in the air, he plans to avoid attacking the Beasties directly as he doesn't want to hurt them—instead he will focus on the Casters. He lands and makes eye contact with the coolheaded Captain standing behind the group.

Sam slams his foot on the ground and three stones the size of golf balls fly up. Sam imbues them with Aura and they shine with energy (Aura control). He states that he's tried doing it without the rocks, or even making the Aura some sort of other element, but he's still working out the kinks. He hits all three in rapid succession at the trio of soldiers turning to him.

One makes contact with the Billdad Caster, who passes out and the Billdad disappears. The second soldier dodges one of the rocks (which zooms past Love). The third hits it with his electrified metal rod.

As the second soldier charges, Sam charges too, and dodges the fireball attacks from the Giant Salamander (speed). Sam charges his baseball bat as the second move he learned, and hits the second soldier with a jab to the stomach that contains very little momentum, but because of the Aura, it blasts away his shirt and renders him unconscious—the Giant Salamander disappears.

Just then the third soldier hits Sam squarely, and it's revealed that Sam has caught the Lightning Rod with his hand, taking the brunt of the electrical current without even wincing (endurance). For the third move, he calls it "just a good, old-fashioned punch!" and knocks out the third soldier.

Love is amazed at his growth, and once again comments about how Sam might be a prodigy. If only he had a cooler head.

### Captain Zebe

Without missing a beat, and his blood running hot, Sam runs full tilt towards the Captain, bat at the ready. From his side he throws the metal bar from the third soldier towards the Captain, and it's deflected off of something invisible. Love shouts that Sam should have listened, that the Captain was just as strong as the dark figure Sam met at the battle of Loveland Castle. The Captain is momentarily taken aback by the fact that the Beastie is talking but growls that Sam's tricks won't work on him.

Just then, Sam is hit with something square in the chest that he couldn't see, stopping his momentum and throwing him back. He rolls on the ground and stops in a crouching position. He activates his Quantum, thinking that whatever it was, he would be able to dodge it if he can see its pathway. The Captain still hasn't moved, but is now smiling.

Sam slams his foot onto the ground again and even larger rocks are imbued with his Aura. He hits them again, and the Captain whistles, "Nice Aura, kid," before the rocks stop just short of him and land on the ground with small explosions. The explosions cause a mini dust screen that distracts the Captain, and from the dust Sam emerges with the powered-up baseball bat. The Captain's eyes widen with excitement as he recognizes that Sam isn't just strong, but a smart fighter too.

Sam swings too high to his amazement, and he internally thinks that his aim was true—what was going on?—right before he's hit ten, no a dozen times in the chest with blows just as powerful as the first one. He flies back and curls on the ground.

_Method six... my Quantum can be beat—invisible attacks._ Sam throws up blood and asks the Captain what the hell his attack method is. The Captain reprimands Sam, saying that this isn't a movie where he explains his technique just so that Sam can find a way around it—that on the battlefield, asymmetry of information is the key to success. As he says this he eyes Love, who is still standing there. He doesn't like the look of Love, and thinks that attacking Sam until he passes out will make Love disappear, as per the terms of a normal Contract. He doesn't know why Sam didn't use his Beasties in the battle—perhaps it was hubris? He wasn't going to wait around to find out.

The Captain apologizes to Sam and says he's unfortunately going to have to hurt him more than he wants. _I'm going to have to break you._ But then he thinks that the kid is far too valuable to kill, and that he was going to recruit him. He crosses his arms and gathers enough Aura to make the ground rumble.

**THWACK!**

The sound of something like a large bottle catching the wind rings in Sam's ear and the Captain is blown back with such force that his jacket is torn to tatters as he flies hard enough into a tree for the top half of it to crack and fall down.

The Captain, through his pain, says with a smile that he knew Love wasn't a normal Beastie. Love walks forward and says that if the Captain won't explain his ability, Love will. The Captain uses wind, but Love reckons it's more complicated than that—the Captain likely creates vacuums to move and push air and other objects in whatever direction he pleases. Captain says that Love is pretty terrifying for breaking down his technique after seeing it used only twice. Love took the same principle, and just enhanced it in the attack that he just used.

Captain then says he will have to pull out the big guns and summons his Spellbook. Love positions his staff at the ready, protecting Sam. Just then, a voice calls out Love's name from the trees, and Bianca emerges.

### The Convoy

Bianca emerges from the trees with the Convoy and more soldiers at her back. She tells Captain Zebe to stand down—these are friends. Love and a now-sitting-up Sam stare blankly at her before Love exclaims, "Bianca!"

Bianca apologizes for the actions of her soldiers—they've been on edge for weeks as they have been chased by the Spirit Storm. As they talk, the Convoy moves forward and picks up the soldiers. They grab onto Sam as well, who struggles. Love tells him it's okay—they will heal and feed him.

Love concedes that he knew it was a possibility that Bianca was leading the main Convoy. Something that didn't sit well with him was the fact that as [TODO]'s pupil, Bianca was more than well-equipped enough to disperse the storm. She was likely the most powerful Caster in all of Ohio.

The Convoy is quite large given the size of the city. About 2,000 Casters and 10,000 normal soldiers in the Cincinnati Rover Guards. Normal human militia protect against humans—a bullet still does the trick, most of the time. Students join as well. When the battle comes, they are instructed not to look at the faces of the Spirits.

Bianca says it's complicated, and that they better move—she would explain on the way. They had to move. Night was coming. Love pulls Sam aside and tells him to get healed, to feed, and to rest. Something wasn't right here.

### Night Falls

Sam is being healed in the back of an open trailer and being fed some real food for the first time in a while. He notices that Bianca looks haggard, scared, and most importantly, not all there. Sam watches Bianca and Love talk, and the conversation is clearly strained. Sam grabs some more food, finds Lucy, jumps in the seat, and tells Dingbelle that he's going to get some shut-eye and to follow the Convoy.

Sam is shaken awake by Captain Zebe. In a panic, Sam reaches for his bat, but Zebe tells him to relax—he's on his side. Sam gets out of Lucy and realizes the Convoy has stopped. It's dark outside, and Sam notices the pink and purples on the horizon, closing in. There is a group surrounding a trailer, with troops protecting it—clearly Bianca's trailer.

Bianca comes out looking distraught—why haven't they moved?! She sees Love and freaks out, saying they have to move, that the Spirit Storm is almost upon them. Love calls her by her first name and her various titles, and says she's the most powerful Caster in the region and that no Spirit Storm is strong enough to sway her branches. Love says, addressing Bianca and the people gathered, that it's time to go home—they have been running for too long. It is time to stand and fight. To arms!

Love summons a bubble that encapsulates the trailer and her guard, and a duel commences.

### The Battle

The Convoy and its troops all start moving to action and Zebe is giving orders. Sam is full of questions. Zebe states that Bianca's family were in the Spirit Storm, and that her husband, who was Mayor of the city, was killed by the invading forces that drove them out in the first place. It's the Red Ghost that Sam saw. Her family being in the Spirit Storm was enough to have her make them run rather than face them. In fact, most people didn't mind this because they saw their own familiar faces in the storm. But they are exhausted and tired of running. Any more and they would fall to the Spirit Storm regardless. Might as well put up a fight while they have the energy and spirit to.

Sam says he understands running from the Spirit Storm, but what caused it? What was powerful and dark enough that not only raised the Spirit Storm, but made it flee the city itself?

Zebe says that's a story for morning's light, if they made it through the night. Zebe tells Sam to find a barricade and to fight if he can, and to hide if he can't. Zebe runs off into the night shouting orders.

There's a flurry of activity as people move to the back of the Convoy and assemble barricades and create fortified positions. Sam is amazed by the number of troops he's seeing, and hunkers down next to a group of kids he thinks are around his age. They ask why he's not in uniform, and state that if he's not a Caster, he needs to find refuge elsewhere. Sam says he is a Caster, just not in the military, and remarks that there are more troops than he was expecting.

A soldier says there are about two thousand Casters and ten thousand normal soldiers in the Cincinnati Rover Guards, and that they were mobilizing just before they were attacked. Sam asks what they were mobilizing for. Another soldier asks if Sam has been living under a rock, before being interrupted by another soldier who is eyeing Sam's bat and states that physical attacks won't work on Spirit Beasties—which is why the normal soldiers are helping build the barricades and then hanging back. Only Spells that are projectile and Aura-based work. Even then you don't really kill them, just... disperse their energy. But if you do that enough, you can break up the storm.

Sam notices all sorts of weaponry being assembled—Caster Arms, a larger version of the Caster Gun. Others have Proton Beam Cannons. There are also Shield Totems being deployed. Various Beasties are contracted as well.

### Spirit Storm

Just then, a hush falls over the crowd. Stars appear above them. Someone says they used to love waiting up to watch the stars emerge—now they feared it. The Spirit Storm emerges in full force. There is shouting here and there, but it's eerily silent. Sam asks what will happen, what do the Spirits do, how do they attack?

Just as he asks this, a Spirit orb shoots forth and hits the line of Casters and a scream rings out. Shield Totems are activated, and both sides begin firing on each other. When a Spell hits a Spirit, it explodes and disappears. Sam is amazed, especially as the other young soldiers begin shooting off their own Spells. Sam, cupping his ears against the sound, asks how long it will take to break up the storm. Another student says there's no guarantee that's even possible, as the Spirit Storm is projected to have more than a million Spirits in it. Sam sees Zebe use his vacuum abilities to literally explode and implode dozens of Spirits at a time.

Just then a soldier is hit through the Shield Totem and falls to the ground beside them, ashen grey and with ectoplasm oozing from his mouth—clearly dead. Someone shouts, "We got some Eddies!" Sam looks up to see that in the storm there are four or five larger lights that are clearly more powerful than the rest. Huge Spells are shot forth from these and hit the line, with enough force that multiple Totems and people are blown back.

Sam says he recognizes Red Ghost from the previous month. A soldier shouts to concentrate the Caster Arms on the Eddies, and they do. But then the Spirit Storm rushes forward. Sam hears Zebe call out, "Ready bayonets!" And the Spirit Storm crashes into the front line. The Shield Totems do a good job of holding them back, but some slip through and many of the shields disappear and the Spirits are upon them.

Their small group is doing well—Beasties are fighting and disappearing as they get destroyed as well. Sam charges up his bat with Aura and destroys a bunch of Spirits with relative ease. One of the female soldiers says not too shabby, maybe he isn't completely useless after all—but this is cut short as a Spirit chain smacks into her, blowing her back with enough force to remove the upper portion of her body.

Standing right in front of them is the Red Ghost upon his horse. The younger soldiers scream and attack, only to be absolutely destroyed by the Red Ghost. Sam in his surprise and anger powers up his bat and jumps to attack, but to his horror the Aura and power of this swing is only enough to crack the forearm of the Red Ghost, who actually gives enough pause to look at the crack and Sam with almost surprise on his skeletal features. Then he hits Sam with his chain and it's done with enough force, in the same spot Orange Eyes hit him. He flies to the ground and everything turns to slow motion.

### Sam's Light

Sam sees more soldiers fall, including one that he faced just a day ago. The situation and pain is so similar to when he faced Orange Eyes that he starts to carry out the same plan in his confusion. He creates an orb of light, small at first, but then larger and larger, until it's the size of a car, then a small house. The Spirits are annoyed by it, but it's just light. The Red Ghost steps forward, unfazed.

Then Sam remembers that it's not just light, but sunlight. And as soon as he thinks this, the light orb turns into a mini sun—powerful enough to make Spirits disappear on the spot, powerful enough for even the Red Ghost to scream as he disappears. The Spirit Storm parts around the false sun and breaks up. Sam throws up a bunch of blood and then passes out.
