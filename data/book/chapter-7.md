## World Building Elements

- Nighttime marches of spirits and dark Beasties during Halloween night, where the Veil was always weakest when it was in place. Now Halloween night also boosts the potency of spirits and Dark Aura ever since the Veil has shattered - so largely it's the same, just more so.

- Most Casters have an affinity to an Aura type, their own personal Aura begins to take the shape of that Aura, and like muscle memory, it solidifies over time. But those that consistently train the basics, and transitions between Aura, prevent that calcification, and those with sufficiently high Aura can calcify multiple parts and develop expertise in multiple areas, although you can never escape the reality and inevitability of an affinity.

## Summary

Love tells Sam that he has to build up his basic physical attributes, and fast, otherwise he will die from basic exposure.

What? No spells? Even before training his Quantum, he needs to get the basics down.

Luckily Love has a portable training kit that he has a patent pending on - guaranteed to train up to P9001 or your money back. This is meant to be a tribute and preview of the labors he will go through. Love says that he can teach him the basics, hell he could stay in school and learn every spell to ever exist, but the true learning will come from the labors he faces in the real world, in the months to come. This is fine by Sam, if he can get by without having to learn actual Spells, that suits him just fine.

Love reveals Sam's current power level is around 1/100 using the Kaleidoscope, which is impressive considering that he's never trained a day in his life, only gotten his ass kicked.

### Training Regimen

**Aura** - Artifact - ghost box Sam has to use his aura to keep it inside, inside is a russian nesting doll, each one has a more powerful spirit beastie in it. First spirit is a Feu Follet that burns Sam's hand unless he protects it using aura.

**Speed** - Snipe hunt, cracked from a golden egg, he catches one colored feather off the snipe, reducing its weight and causing it to go faster. Stops after one feather.

**Durability** - Spell, a basic energy spell that Sam thinks he's learning how to use a defensive spell to block against, but it was meant to be impossible to block and Sam is actually meant to be hit again and again. Meanwhile Love is using spells to change the environment a la DBZ time temple. Cold leading into fireballs, over and over again.

**Strength** - Love hides M's spellbook deep underground, and Sam has to dig underneath it to find it, where he encounters the skeleton of a Slide Rock Bolter. Love looks down and speaks of Bolter like it's a legendary beastie, probably only a handful left in the world, which is probably a good thing since they are such powerful allies. In order to get out of the hole, Sam needs to jump out of it - no climbing. Frustration with being too weak, how will he ever defeat something like Mothman or the casters that control him? How can he ever match up against casters as powerful as the Rainbow Wizard or even more powerful if he's here digging through the ground? Just as his frustration reaches its maximum, he breaks through into the cave.

### Healing & Mechanics

In order to help him heal, Love shows him how to make medicinal potion from a Black Willow Tree's bark.

Love tells Sam: "You can't heal yourself, it's like something that's broken trying to fix itself, but the mechanism to fix itself is broken. There are ways around that, like aura sutures and life leech, but otherwise you have to rely on other people, which is an important lesson to learn."

### Progress

4 weeks has gone by since training has begun, and Love tests him using the power ranking device. Reveals that Sam has made significant progress, and is at roughly 1/2000 in just 4 weeks of pure physical training. Did Sam not feel his body begging, calling out to be trained? He tells Sam that the next part he has to do on his own - he must go off into the woods and come up with one original move, that's all based on everything he's learned so far through his blood, sweat, and tears.

### Halloween Night

At some point, Halloween comes, we don't dive too deeply into it this book, or this year rather, but Love does see something - a parade of spirits and other things through the forest that are wearing pumpkins for heads. He tells Sam to ignore it and keep quiet. There are many parades across the countryside that night. Before the veil was shattered, it was thinnest during Halloween. Now that the veil is shattered, it's one of the darkest nights of the year. They are safe, but only just.

### Notes

- Throughout all of this, Love is impressed with Sam's progress, saying to himself that he was able to do in a single month what took most casters years to do, if they ever even could accomplish it.
- Dingbelle sits by doing the training with Sam as comic relief.
- Sam goes off, and he trains off screen. It's important we don't show this, and that it remains a mystery to the reader, as it will be demonstrated in the next chapter.
