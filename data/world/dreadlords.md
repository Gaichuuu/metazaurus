# Dreadlords

_Organizations_

Hidden like the Akatsuki, they need to show up periodically and be responsible for the chaos Sam faces throughout the story.

## Structure

- Need a meeting of the Dreadlords, an entire chapter dedicated to it fairly early on
- They need to be countered by the good Evergreens
- There are Evergreens who are neutral

## Regional

- Only the American-based Dreadlords should play a role in this story
- The others should be hinted at for other regions
- The meeting ends with Indrid saying he is going to raise a new Dreadlord, and this is the Dreadlord that creates problems for Sam and group
