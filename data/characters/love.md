## Role in Story

Love is Sam's mentor.

## Physical Description

A large frog dressed as a wizard. About five feet tall, slimy hands and body, amphibian features with a croaking voice. Wears ancient human clothing older than the oak in his castle. Carries a staff topped with a beautiful blue and gold orb (Forest God Amber). Has a palm shaped like a heart and frog-like lips pulled back in a maddening smile.

## Occupation

Guardian of Loveland Castle.

## Habits/Mannerisms

- Uses croaking sounds and foreign exclamations ("Daingead!")
- Whacks things with his staff for emphasis
- Paces while thinking
- Makes "moist plop" sounds when moving
