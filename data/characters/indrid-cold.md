## Role in Story

The primary antagonist.

## Goal

Indrid's goals are complex, but they are all driven by one thing: finding a true "god." His search, his journey, his actions, all with the intent to draw out or find some real "god" walking the earth. Magic is the remaining tools leftover by now absent gods, and Indrid feels the perpetual orphan.

If he can find one of these original gods, he can find out what the true purpose of everything is. Indrid Cold's goal is to knock on the door, and see who answers. This is why he is amassing an army.

## Physical Description

Physically attractive to both men and women, despite his features being quite off-putting at a glance. Over 6'5, like many Evergreen Casters, he normally wears a suit as though he takes his physical appearance very seriously.

He is white, but with a touch of olive to his skin that looks like its been unattended by the sun for far too long, indicating that he's somewhat foreign. There is a slight accent on his tongue, but it seems like it is seemingly from everywhere and nowhere all at once. He has almond colored eyes, with deep gold that is lit from within.

Always a smile on his face, a smile that seems almost unnaturally large. He is quick to laughter, and the laughter appears genuine.

Despite his attractiveness and his mirth, looking at him for too long is enough to drive a sane person insane.

## Personality

Indrid Cold, at first glance, appears to be your standard, comically evil villain. True evil can take several forms, but it is often understanding that what you are doing wrong is wrong, but still doing it anyways. This type of evil is rare, and most acts or the people who commit them, that are considered evil are usually done with good intentions. Evil acts done in the name of good.

We may be quick to judge Indrid Cold as the purer form of evil, but he is in fact quite human. He is so old, and his reasons for doing things so deep and alien to the normal human with normal problems (and dreams and desires), that the only thing we can do is think of him as evil. To consider the possibility of something else would drive us crazy in its complexity and meaning.
