# Dark Caster

**Dark Caster [INTJ - Foil]**<br>
_18 • Mystery_

## Role in Story

This is the alter ego of Adam Ackler. Shrouded in mystery and a black robe, he wears 10 rings on his fingers from the black pallet and his eyes shine blue from beneath the hood (the light reflecting off his glasses).

## Occupation

Rogue Caster

## Artifacts

- 10 rings from the black pallet

## Notes

This is Adam Ackler's secret identity. The reveal that he's the dark figure Sam has been chasing is a major plot point.
