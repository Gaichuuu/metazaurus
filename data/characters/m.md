## Book References

**Chapter 1** - Appears in Point Pleasant from the northeast after the Veil shatters. Makes Cryptid-shaped pastries and uses magic for household chores. Created Point Pleasant's barrier within 3 days. Aware of Sam's bullying but lets him handle it. When Mothman returns, catches Sam falling from the exploding radio tower. Breaks eye contact with Mothman to push Sam through a Door to the Pathways. Faces Mothman with a smile, tears out his final Spellbook page, and releases Mothman from its Contract with eldritch green flame: "I release you from your Contract, old friend." Dies in the resulting explosion.

**Chapter 2** - Deceased. The Headcaster reveals M was his teacher "a long time ago" and is not on the list of thirteen known Evergreen Casters. Sam carries M's crimson Spellbook, which now appears blank with the golden "M" decoration missing.

**Chapter 4** - Love shows reverence for M's Spellbook, calling it one of the most valuable in existence. The Spellbook's transformation (blank pages, missing M) suggests magical upheaval from M's death.

**Chapter 5** - Love finds Spells in M's Spellbook that Sam can access. The Spellbook is revealed to have Sam's name on it, not M's.

**Chapter 7** - Love hides M's Spellbook underground as part of Sam's solo training.
