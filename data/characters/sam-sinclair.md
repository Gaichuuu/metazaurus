## Book References

**Chapter 1** - Age 8 at the 2021 baseball game; demonstrates Intuition for the first time seeing the paths a baseball will take. Mothman appears and kills everyone except Sam and Adam. Adopted by M. At 16, bullied daily by Stefan's gang. Adam and Rose leave for Quimblys. At 18, photographs Spirits at the CryptidFM Tower barrier. Mothman returns and destroys Point Pleasant. M sacrifices himself, pushing Sam through a Door to the Pathways.

**Chapter 2** - Emerges from the Pathways in Unity City a week later. Captured by Quimblys agents at Georgetown Tobacco. Meets the Rainbow Wizard (Cassius Cane) and learns Adam has disappeared. Imprisoned in the Tower. Uses M's Spellbook to escape. Breaks his cage bars, glides down, and flees. Discovers Dingbelle living in Lucy's engine and forms an accidental Contract. Drives west toward Loveland Castle after hearing Mothman was sighted there.

**Chapter 3** - Arrives at Loveland Castle. Witnesses the Dark Caster defeat Mothman with a blackhole spell from a Caster Gun. Catches the Dark Caster when he collapses from exhaustion. Enters the castle to escape the Spirit Storm. Battles Love in the entrance hall, using his Intuition to counter six different attack types. Defeated and knocked unconscious by fire.

**Chapter 4** - Wakes up healed by Love's saliva bandages. Eats a Sinclair Sandwich created from worms by the Platter Adorn. Learns Love's name and that the creature he fought wasn't actually Mothman. Love demonstrates the six ways to defeat Abstract Powers by beating Sam with his staff. Discovers Mothman is still alive above the castle.

**Chapter 5** - Love teaches Sam about Veil Golems, Creation Clay, and the dark forces amassing in the North. Learns the fundamental theory of magic: willpower over conduits. Performs the Luz Exercise successfully on his first try (what takes average Casters half a year). Borrows Solomon's Spellbook for an invisibility spell. Uses the Squatch Poncho during the castle siege.

**Chapter 6** - Attacked by Orange Eyes, the Beastie Caster. Punched into a tree and nearly killed. Love telepathically guides him. Performs a brilliant Luz Exercise to signal Love, then swings his bat to hit Love's magical firework, destroying Orange Eyes. Discovers his Spellbook has his name on it, not M's. The mysterious dark figures in a black car appear. Love offers to train Sam for one month.

**Chapter 7** - Love begins intensive training with the P9001 Kit. Sam trains all four attributes: Aura, Speed, Durability, and Strength. Love measures Sam's Power Level at 1/100 with the Kaleidoscope. Learns to make Black Willow Tree medicine. Observes the Halloween Night parade of Pumpkin Spirits. After 4 weeks reaches 1/2,000. Love hides M's Spellbook underground and sends Sam into the forest alone for 2 weeks of solo training.

**Chapter 8** - Emerges from solo training with new techniques. Confronted by Cincinnati Convoy soldiers attempting to conscript him. Defeats them. Duels Captain Zebe, discovering his Quantum can be beaten by invisible vacuum attacks. Bianca arrives and stops the fight. During the Spirit Storm battle, creates a massive light orb that evolves into sunlight powerful enough to destroy the Red Ghost and break up the storm. Collapses and coughs up blood.
