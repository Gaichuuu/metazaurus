## Role in Story

Adam is our anti-hero, the foil and challenge and rival and best friend of our hero, Sam Sinclair.

## Goal

Adam’s goal is to gain as much power as he possibly can in order to prevent what happened to him as a child from ever happening again. Hence, when it did happen again while he was away training at Quimblys, he decided to leave and strike out on his own, not satisfied with his progress (we assume that he coldly went off, not caring, but it’s revealed by Rose that this is not true).

It is for this reason why he seeks out the aid of the person he believes to be the most powerful Evergreen Caster, Indrid Cold, although Rose believes it’s because he actually wants to defeat him because he blames him for the Mothman attacks.

_Main Goal: Kill Indrid Cold_

## Physical Description

Adam is 6’0, slender build, and wears professional attire. He has jet-black hair and circular glasses "a little too reminiscent of Harry Potter to be entirely an accident." He is white with blue eyes, and freckles just barely visible on his cheeks and nose.

Despite his nerdy appearance, he was “only ever bullied once” and has become somewhat of a local star in both sports and academics.

Girls pine over him for his cool, dark looks.

## Personality

Adam is quiet and calm tempered, to the point of mistakenly coming off as cold, or that he’s too cool for school. When he does smile, it’s a revelation, and it’s a wonder he doesn’t do it more often. The loss of his parents, family, etc… weigh heavily on him, and he has taken that pain and turned it inward. He is incredibly hardworking, and trains and reads constantly, although to what end and what purpose he never publicly reveals, not even to those closest to him.

He views casting and beasties as tools, although he doesn’t particularly hate beasties despite how it might come off.

He wants to use Indrid, but not rely on his help entirely. Hence why Adam is so reluctant to use the sigil Indrid granted him.

Adam is willing to do adult things that surprise “real adults” such as risking his life, and protecting others.

He has a disdain for the other followers of Indrid, the Sigil Casters, for the weakness and reliance on Indrid’s power, and of course is disgusted by their brutality and surface-level evil.

## Background

Descended from the bloodline of King Arthur and Morgan La Fay by Morgause, sister of Morgan La Fay. And so, he is of the same Kingly blood as Sam, but the magical power in his blood comes from Morgan La Fay.

## Internal Conflicts

Adam must train under the one he believes to be responsible for the tragedy that defines Adam’s life, Indrid Cold. His goal is to use this training to become powerful enough to defeat Indrid.

Adam’s strength (and weakness) lies in the fact that he struck out on his own, and through his isolation he seeks to save those who in the end think Adam abandoned them.
