# Outskirts of Cincinnati, Ohio

_Southern Ohio • United States_

## Overview

The rural country south of Cincinnati where most of the story takes place after Sam leaves Point Pleasant.

## Geography

**Loveland Castle**: A European-style stone folly built in the 1920s. The Little Miami River curls around it. Protected by serious magical wards. Spirits can't cross the bridge. A flooded parking lot at the base has become a marsh.

**Sentry Hill**: Off Highway 48, overlooking the valley.

**Rivers**: Little Miami runs past the castle. The Ohio River runs along the southern edge.

**Marshlands**: A big marsh fed by the river, sinking into the side of Loveland Castle. Fog rests on it, ice starts forming on the surface as winter closes in.

**Roads**: Highway 48 past Sentry Hill. Loveland Madeira Road along the Little Miami. Ohio River Scenic Byway heading back toward Point Pleasant.

## Climate

October-November: Weather is getting colder. Still reaches 80s during the day, but nighttime drops to the 20s. By November, highs are in the 50s with lows in the 20s.

## Flora

**Trees**: Oak-hickory, maple-beech-birch

**Medicinal Plants**: Black willow tree

## Human Elements

**Abandoned River Side Cafe**: A diner along the road. Marquee sign says "Gone Fishin'" with some letters missing.

**Cincinnati Convoy**: A massive mobile caravan (trailers, classic cars, diesel trucks) circling the dead city. About 2,000 Casters and 10,000 normal soldiers in the Cincinnati Rover Guards.
