# Point Pleasant

_West Virginia • United States_

## Overview

Sam and Adam's home for the decade following the Veil shattering.

## Geography

Small town in West Virginia. Surrounded by the "Wilds," unprotected land beyond M's barrier where Spirits and Beasties roam freely.

**The Barrier**: M's magical Veil protecting the town. Had some minor flaws that let a few Spirits slip through.

**CryptidFM Radio Tower**: The town's radio station.

## Climate

Late summer to early fall.

---

## Human Elements

**Baxter Middle-High School**: Where Sam went to school. Has a Little League field with bleachers. Indrid Cold's wanted posters are plastered all over them.

**Mrs. Chapel's House**: Where Rose and Adam said goodbye to Sam before leaving for Quimblys.

**M's House**: Home to Sam, Adam, and M for ten years.
